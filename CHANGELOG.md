## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-14

### Added

- Native Replay tab for DeepSeek Harness sessions.
- Real-event timeline, playback, search, filtering, history loading, and event details.
- Standalone interactive HTML export styled after the official Trajectory surface.
- Share selection that excludes system context and assistant reasoning.
- Redaction of local user paths and common credential patterns.
- English and Chinese product UI.
- Bilingual English and Simplified Chinese documentation.
- Privacy unit tests, community health files, issue forms, pull request template, and CI.
- Real, privacy-safe Harness screenshots and a concise project overview graphic.
