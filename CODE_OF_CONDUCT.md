# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Protect other people's privacy and security.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and improve.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently ban participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
